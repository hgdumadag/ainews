⚡ 30-Second Executive Summary

The week's dominant signal was the escalation of the AI-infrastructure financing arms race: NVIDIA assembled six Wall Street firms to mobilize over $500B of third-party capital (Aug 10), while Anthropic (with Macquarie and GIC), CoreWeave's $104B backlog, and Cisco's $9.3B in AI orders showed demand and capital intensity concentrating vendor and financing risk. The biggest opportunity is enterprise AI moving into governed, at-scale deployment — IBM joined OpenAI's Elite tier to embed frontier models across core finance, procurement, and HR operations, and Databricks raised $5B at a $190B valuation on a $7B+ run-rate data-and-AI platform. The biggest risk and governance implication is twofold: OpenAI's GPT-5.6-Cyber narrows the cyber-defense window for everyone, and a frontier-vendor safety-leadership exodus put AI-agent controls and third-party governance squarely on the board agenda. Recommended discussion: whether the enterprise treats AI-infrastructure financing concentration and frontier-vendor governance as material third-party and financial risks.
