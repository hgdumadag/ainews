Executive Watchlist

  • 🔵 Monitor — Whether model price reductions translate into lower total workflow cost after supervision, integration, and rework.
  • 🟡 Discuss — A deliberate portfolio of hosted, sovereign, and open-weight models with documented switching paths.
  • 🔴 Act — Inventory agent permissions, network egress, credential reach, sandbox assumptions, and human takeover points.
  • 🟣 Audit/assurance question — Can management evidence that every production agent follows approved policy under normal, edge-case, and adversarial conditions?
  • 🩵 Policy/governance update — Add AI incident notice, evaluation evidence, data provenance, audit rights, and regulatory-change clauses to vendor standards.

Questions for Management

  1. Which enterprise agents can take external actions, and what independent evidence confirms that their boundaries hold?
  2. Where are we locked into one model, cloud, chip ecosystem, or deployment policy—and what is the tested exit route?
  3. Can each AI use case trace its training, retrieval, and uploaded content to lawful and contractually permitted sources?
  4. How are workforce plans distinguishing role redesign, reskilling, redeployment, and control ownership from simple cost reduction?
  5. What assurance should the Audit Committee request before agent use expands into finance, customer, employee, or safety-critical processes?
