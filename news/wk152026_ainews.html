⚡ 30-Second Executive Summary
Anthropic dominated the week on infrastructure and products: on April 6, the company disclosed its annual revenue run rate has surpassed $30 billion while signing a deal with Google and Broadcom for 3.5 gigawatts of TPU compute starting 2027; on April 8, it launched Claude Managed Agents at $0.08/session hour with Notion, Rakuten, and Asana among the first customers; and on April 10, it inked a separate multibillion-dollar cloud deal with CoreWeave. On April 9, Amazon CEO Andy Jassy published his annual shareholder letter disclosing — for the first time — that AWS AI services have reached a $15 billion annualized revenue run rate and defending $200 billion in 2026 capital expenditure. Also April 9: Meta and CoreWeave expanded their agreement to $21 billion through 2032. Meanwhile, Meta launched Muse Spark on April 8, the first model from its newly formed Superintelligence Labs, scoring 52 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index.
